1.2IBSL gives you personal access to this content for your own learning during the programme.

2. Use of content

You must not:

  • copy, screenshot, photograph, download, print or record programme content, except where the portal provides a print or save function for your own work or your team’s Capstone work
  • share programme content with anyone outside your cohort
  • publish programme content, or enter it into any AI tool or other public platform

3. Account access

3.1Your login is personal to you. You must not share it or allow anyone else to use it.

3.2Your name and email address may appear on portal pages to identify your account.

4. Live sessions

4.1Each session link is for your use only. You must not forward or share it.

4.2You may record a session only with IBSL’s written permission.

5. Session recordings

5.1IBSL records live sessions. By accepting these terms, you consent to your voice, image, name and session contributions being recorded.

5.2IBSL uses recordings to support participants who missed a session and to review and improve delivery.

6. Confidentiality

6.1Keep confidential anything that other participants share in sessions, group work or the Capstone, including information about them and their organisations.

6.2Share information about your own organisation only where you are entitled to do so.

7. Your work

7.1You keep ownership of the content you write.

7.2IBSL, your facilitators and the Capstone assessors may use your work to deliver, assess and certify the programme.

8. Personal information

8.1IBSL processes your name, contact details, portal activity, attendance, submissions and scores to deliver, administer, assess and certify the programme. It does so in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013.

8.2To access or correct your personal information, email projects@ibsleadership.com.

9. Breach

If you breach these terms, IBSL may suspend or end your access to the portal and live sessions. IBSL may also take any other action available to it by law.

10.2These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.
