# The Strategic Unclogging Clinic

Returning to an original work-based challenge, identifying the performance clog, selecting the corresponding Strategic Unclogging Lever and applying Review–Realign–Restore within the capstone organisation.

## Section learning outcomes

-   Classify an original organisational challenge using the Seven Performance Clogs and select an appropriate established Unclogging Lever.

-   Develop a collective executive response through Review–Realign–Restore, addressing people performance and organisational performance.

## Facilitator Guidance The Clog Clinic

At the beginning of CESI, each group identified recurring work-based organisational challenges drawn from its members’ professional experiences. These challenges informed the simulated capstone organisation. Participants must now return to that original list, select one challenge and apply the S2R diagnostic and intervention tools they have learned.

Teach the sequence clearly: original challenge → identify the clog → select the lever → Review–Realign–Restore. The initial classification is a working diagnosis; REVIEW checks it against performance evidence before the team commits to action. If REVIEW reveals a different underlying condition, the team must correct the clog and lever selection.

Do not allow a familiar symptom to become an unsupported diagnosis. The group must explain why its selected clog fits the observed behaviours and organisational conditions. During REALIGN, require implications for both people and organisational performance. During RESTORE, require a specific application of the selected lever, named executive ownership, completion timing and evidence of recovery.

**Suggested timing.** 45 minutes: 5 minutes challenge selection, 10 minutes clog and lever selection, 10 minutes REVIEW, 10 minutes REALIGN and 10 minutes RESTORE. Adjust discussion time to the needs of the cohort.

## 5.5 — Diagnostic Quality Check

Before the group finalises its response, test it against four criteria:

1\. The original challenge is linked to a specific strategic result and supported by realistic performance evidence.

2\. The selected clog describes an underlying organisational condition rather than simply repeating the symptom.

3\. The lever addresses the confirmed clog, and REALIGN considers both people and organisational performance.

4\. The RESTORE intervention is specific, owned, time-bound and supported by evidence that will show whether performance is recovering.

If any criterion is not met, return to the relevant step and refine the response.

## 5.6 — Capstone Portfolio Output

The group submits one Strategic Unclogging Clinic Record containing:

1\. The original work-based challenge selected from its pre-CESI preparation.

2\. The identified Seven Performance Clog and reason for the diagnosis.

3\. The selected Seven Strategic Unclogging Lever and its intended purpose.

4\. The completed REVIEW, REALIGN and RESTORE responses, with executive ownership and evidence of recovery.

The completed record is incorporated into the existing capstone portfolio.

## Facilitator Closing Note Application

Invite each group to explain in one minute: the original challenge, the confirmed clog, the selected lever and the collective leadership response. Ask: “How has your understanding of this challenge changed since you first identified it before CESI?”

Close by reinforcing the integrated discipline: recognise the challenge, identify the clog, select the suitable lever, and apply Review–Realign–Restore to confirm the diagnosis, coordinate leadership decisions and verify recovery across people and organisational performance.
