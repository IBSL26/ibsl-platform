The client services company of Unit 3, part 2.2. Its four Objectives and eight Key Results are carried over unchanged.

**Step 1 · Individual Reality Check.** Each leader names one condition per checkpoint for each Key Result, from their own role. Shown for Key Result 1a, Boundaries:
- Chief Operating Officer: "Handovers between functions are completed inside four hours."
- Chief Commercial Officer: "Account managers can be reached by the service team during client hours."

**Step 2 · Executive Consensus.** The agreed conditions:

**Objective 1 · Deliver consistently fast and effortless service experiences for customers**

*KR 1a · Reduce average response time to customer requests from 12 hours to 4 hours by 30 June 2027*
- Arena: Clients continue to rank speed of response among their top three reasons for choosing a service partner.
- Boundaries: Request volumes stay within the capacity of the service team, and handovers between Commercial, Operations and Customer Service are completed inside four hours.
- Competition: A four-hour response remains faster than rival firms and clients' in-house options provide.
- Value Proposition: Faster response is delivered with no loss in the quality of the answer the client receives.

*KR 1b · Increase customer requests resolved at first contact from 60% to 85% by 30 September 2027*
- Arena: Clients continue to value a request settled in a single contact.
- Boundaries: Front-line staff hold the authority, the client information and the system access to resolve requests without referral.
- Competition: Resolution at first contact remains something clients cannot obtain faster from a self-service or automated alternative.
- Value Proposition: Requests closed at first contact stay closed; repeat contacts on the same request do not rise.

**Objective 2 · Build a high-speed execution engine that moves quickly from insight to action**

*KR 2a · Reduce cross-functional decision time from 5 days to 1 day by 30 June 2027*
- Arena: Faster internal decisions shorten the time clients wait for an answer or a change.
- Boundaries: Decision rights are delegated to the cross-functional forum, and governance and contract-approval rules allow a one-day decision.
- Competition: Speed of decision remains an advantage clients notice when they compare the company with larger providers.
- Value Proposition: One-day decisions stay consistent with enterprise priorities, so that speed produces no conflicting commitments to clients.

*KR 2b · Reduce recurring management reports from 24 to 10 by 31 March 2027*
- Arena: No report removed carries information that account teams rely on to serve key accounts.
- Boundaries: The ten remaining reports meet every regulatory, audit and board reporting obligation.
- Competition: The leadership time released is spent on client-facing action.
- Value Proposition: The ten reports give leaders the evidence needed to keep service consistent across teams.

**Objective 3 · Create a culture of shared ownership across functions for strategic priorities**

*KR 3a · Increase enterprise priorities with a named cross-functional owner from 40% to 100% by 31 March 2027*
- Arena: The priorities being assigned are the ones that carry the client outcomes in the SiP statement.
- Boundaries: Each named owner has the mandate and the capacity to lead across functions, and reporting lines and incentives support it.
- Competition: The company retains the experienced leaders it needs as owners through the period.
- Value Proposition: Shared ownership strengthens one joined-up service, and accountability for each key account remains clear to the client.

*KR 3b · Reduce issues escalated to the executive team from 30 to 10 per quarter by 30 September 2027*
- Arena: Client-affecting issues are resolved faster at the level where they arise.
- Boundaries: Managers below executive level hold the decision authority and the information to resolve issues, and escalation rules for regulatory matters stay intact.
- Competition: Issues resolved below executive level are resolved at least as fast as clients expect from an alternative provider.
- Value Proposition: A lower count reflects issues resolved; no unresolved issue is held back from the executive team.

**Objective 4 · Strengthen enterprise performance through scalable and efficient operations**

*KR 4a · Improve operating margin from 14% to 18% by 31 December 2027*
- Arena: Clients continue to pay current price levels for fast, consistent service, and demand from loyal client segments holds.
- Boundaries: Salary, input and regulatory costs stay within the planning range, and scalable service delivery models are in place by mid-2027.
- Competition: Competitors and lower-cost alternatives force no price reduction in the core client segments.
- Value Proposition: Margin improves through efficiency and scale; no saving reduces the service quality clients choose the company for.

*KR 4b · Reduce leadership time spent on low-value projects from 25% to 10% by 30 June 2027*
- Arena: The projects classed as low-value are low-value to clients as well as to the company.
- Boundaries: The company can exit or decline those projects within existing contract terms.
- Competition: Declining low-value work gives competitors no entry point into key accounts.
- Value Proposition: The leadership time released is invested in fast, consistent and effortless service for the clients who rely on the company first.
